Questions, answered

How much is a bus from Nairobi to Busia? +

Fares generally range between Ksh 1,500 and Ksh 2,000 one way, depending on season and demand.

What does the Nairobi–Kampala route cost? +

The cross-border run is Ksh 3,500 (about UGX 120,000) each way.

Is there Wi-Fi on board? +

Yes — every Grand Bus coach has Wi-Fi on board, along with a toilet, so long overnight routes are easier to manage.

Do I need to print my ticket? +

No. Grand Bus issues e-tickets — just bring the confirmation on your phone to the terminal.

How do I book? +

Use the search or any "Book" button to pick your route, date, seats and passengers, then confirm straight over WhatsApp.
